How to Use

  1. Drag & Drop: Drag Pokémon sounds into the six slots.
  2. Fill All Slots: All 6 must be filled to enable controls.
  3. Play Battle! Hit Play to enjoy your tune!
  4. Controls:
    • ▶ Play
    • ■ Stop
    • ⏮ Rewind
    • ⏭ Fast Forward
  5. Master Volume: Use the slider to adjust volume.
  6. Remove: Click 'reset' (or 'X' on a slot) to remove a sound.

Note: You might need to interact with the page to enable sound in your browser.
